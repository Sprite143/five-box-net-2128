Polyomino box nets

Five-Box Net 2128

One polyomino of 2,128 squares that folds into five different boxes, using folds along grid lines only. Choose a box, then fold. Each colour is one face of the chosen box. Step-by-step mode folds one fold line at a time, starting from one end of the strip, turning each flat panel as one rigid piece: the motion a rigid-paper simulation checked for collisions, with none found.

Checks

The net is a single connected strip of 2,128 unit squares, with no holes and no two squares meeting only at a corner. Every box with surface area 2,128 (there are 28) was tested with separate fold checkers, including the checker written by the authors of the three-box paper. All agree that it folds into 2×4×176, 4×8×86, 4×14×56, 4×16×50 and 4×26×32, and into none of the other 23. In every fold, neighbouring squares stay joined and the box surface is covered exactly once. The step-by-step folding shown here was simulated with rigid panels, one fold line at a time in the same order, checking every half degree: no collisions in any of the five boxes.

Drag to turn the model; scroll or pinch to zoom. The animation cycles through the five boxes.
